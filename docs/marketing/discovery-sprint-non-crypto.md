# Non-Crypto ICP Discovery Sprint

This is the 2-week validation sprint for Singularity's revised primary ICP: high-quality, mission-driven projects outside crypto with no existing token, struggling to raise through traditional channels.

Run this sprint before scaling outbound or rewriting the live landing page.

## Sprint Objective

Learn whether non-crypto founders will commit to a public mission launch when Singularity handles the token and crypto mechanics end-to-end.

Book founder calls, not signups.

## Timeline

| Week | Focus | Targets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Week 1 | Source and score 20 leads, contact 15, book 3 calls | 20 leads scored, 15 contacted |
| Week 2 | Contact remaining leads, complete 5 calls, evaluate pass/fail | 5 calls completed, go/no-go decision |

## Lead Criteria

Include only projects that match all of these:

- Mission-driven project outside crypto (or crypto-adjacent but no live token)
- No existing token and not mid-token launch
- Visible proof of work: demo, product, repo, waitlist, or active community
- Founder building in public on X or has a reachable contact surface
- Signs of fundraising difficulty: slow raise, grant rejection, bootstrapped past runway, public posts about capital needs

Exclude:

- Projects with a live token or active token launch
- Crypto-native teams primarily seeking liquidity or exchange listings
- Idea-stage teams with no proof of work
- Teams that need equity-only institutional capital and will not consider community-backed funding
- Anonymous accounts with no trust surface

## Source Mix (20 leads)

| Bucket | Target count | Where to look |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| VC-rejected / slow raise signals | 5 | Founders posting about fundraising struggles, indie SaaS building in public |
| Open source / public goods | 5 | GitHub trending, awesome-list maintainers, OSS with sponsor gaps |
| Mission-first indie products | 5 | Climate, education, health, creative tools, Product Hunt makers |
| Warm intros | 5 | Non-crypto angels, former operators, journalists covering underrated builders |

See [`lead-sourcing-and-tracker.md`](lead-sourcing-and-tracker.md) for full source buckets and tracker fields.

## Outbound Angle

Use only the **Stuck fundraising** angle from [`outbound-message-tests.md`](outbound-message-tests.md) for this sprint. Do not mix angles until pass/fail is decided.

## Call Script Additions

After standard discovery questions from the outbound doc, ask:

1. Have you tried raising through angels, VCs, or grants? What happened?
2. Would you launch a public mission market if Singularity handled wallets, tokens, and the crypto layer?
3. What would make you uncomfortable about a community-backed funding launch?
4. Do you have a community or audience that would back the mission publicly?

## Pass / Fail Criteria

The sprint **passes** if all of these are true after 5 completed calls:

| Criterion | Threshold |
| --- | --- |
| Launch willingness | ≥2 founders would launch publicly if Singularity runs token mechanics |
| Specific use of funds | ≥3 founders name specific 30–60 day work to fund |
| No existing token | 100% of leads have no live token and are not mid-token launch |
| Design partner candidates | ≥2 founders convert to design partner candidates |

The sprint **fails** if:

- Founders only want VC intros or equity SAFEs with no willingness to launch publicly
- Legal/compliance objections block every conversation with no path forward
- Zero founders would co-market a public launch

## Go / No-Go After Sprint

### If pass

- Proceed with full GTM doc stack (already updated in repo)
- Recruit 2 design partners from sprint calls
- Begin white-glove launch prep per [`design-partner-launch-playbook.md`](design-partner-launch-playbook.md)
- Validate non-crypto landing copy variant on calls before shipping to live site

### If fail

- Narrow to open-source / public-goods only and rerun 10 calls
- Or revisit secondary ICP: pre-token crypto-adjacent projects without existing tokens
- Do not scale outbound or x-reply-agent until a segment passes

## Daily Routine

1. Add 3-5 raw leads from non-crypto source buckets.
2. Score each lead using the ICP rubric in [`ideal-customer-profile.md`](ideal-customer-profile.md).
3. Keep only leads scoring 13 or higher.
4. Contact 2-3 founders per day with the stuck-fundraising angle.
5. Log every reply with response labels from [`outbound-message-tests.md`](outbound-message-tests.md).
6. Ask every founder who replies for one referral.
