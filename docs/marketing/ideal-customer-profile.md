# Singularity Ideal Customer Profile

This document defines the first customer segment for Singularity's product-market fit test.

## Primary ICP

The first users should be public crypto projects, starting with Solana projects, that already have a mission and need to fund contributors or early work.

Singularity should not begin by selling to passive investors. Investors need credible missions, public launches, and proof that projects can attract builders and supporters. Projects create that supply.

## Best-Fit Project

A best-fit first project has these traits:

- The founder or team is already building in public on X.
- The project has a concrete mission that can be understood in one sentence.
- There is visible proof of work: demo, GitHub, waitlist, community, product screenshots, shipped contracts, or public roadmap.
- The team needs funding for contributors, bounties, audits, ecosystem growth, content, integrations, infrastructure, research, or launch support.
- The founder is willing to co-market and explain the mission publicly.
- The project is crypto-native enough to understand token markets, community participation, and wallet-based governance.
- The project is early enough to need distribution, but mature enough that supporters can evaluate it.

## Strongest Segments

### 1. Contributor-Funded Builder Teams

Projects with clear work to fund but no clean mechanism for contributor payouts.

Common signals:

- Hiring contributors or community operators.
- Posting bounties, grants, quests, or open tasks.
- Saying they need help with growth, integrations, audits, or dev work.
- Running work through Discord, DMs, or manual grants.

Primary message:

> Singularity lets you launch a mission market and route reserved treasury supply to builders through onchain funding requests.

### 2. Hackathon And Grant Alumni

Projects that have proof of work and momentum, but still need post-demo capital and community.

Common signals:

- Recent hackathon finalist, winner, or demo day participant.
- Grant recipient or grant applicant.
- Public roadmap with unfunded next milestones.
- Founder is actively posting progress.

Primary message:

> You already have a mission people can understand. Singularity can help turn that mission into a market with builder funding built in.

### 3. Pre-Token Communities

Projects with active communities that have not launched a token yet.

Common signals:

- Community waitlist, Discord, Telegram, or X following.
- Founder talks about token timing, launch, decentralization, grants, or ecosystem contributors.
- Team wants community participation without looking like a pure speculation launch.

Primary message:

> Most launchpads stop at distribution. Singularity helps you launch with treasury allocation and contributor funding visible from day one.

## Exclusions

Do not prioritize these projects in the first PMF cycle:

- Idea-stage projects with no public proof of work.
- Anonymous accounts with no trust surface.
- Teams only asking for passive liquidity, token hype, or exchange attention.
- Mature projects that already have venture funding, launch infrastructure, and contributor programs.
- Projects whose mission is too vague to explain without a long pitch.
- Projects that cannot or will not co-market.

## Qualification Questions

Use these questions in founder calls:

1. What work would move the mission forward in the next 30-60 days?
2. Who should be paid to do that work?
3. How are you funding contributors, bounties, audits, or ecosystem work today?
4. Where does that process feel slow, political, opaque, or manual?
5. Would you publicly launch a mission page if Singularity helped with positioning and distribution?
6. Would you ask your community to back the mission or participate in funding decisions?
7. Who are two other founders with the same problem?

## Scoring Rubric

Score every lead from 0-3 on each dimension.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mission clarity | Vague idea | Understandable after explanation | Clear one-line mission | Memorable and public-facing |
| Proof of work | None | Early concept | Demo, repo, or community | Shipped product or strong traction |
| Funding pain | None | Future concern | Active contributor need | Urgent funded-work bottleneck |
| Founder energy | Passive | Interested | Responsive and thoughtful | Willing to co-market quickly |
| Community surface | None | Small audience | Active X or Discord | Audience likely to take action |
| Crypto fit | Unclear | Crypto-adjacent | Token/community aware | Strong fit for mission markets |

Prioritize leads scoring 13 or higher. Only take calls below that threshold if the founder has a direct relationship or unusual strategic value.

## First-Cycle Goal

The first ICP cycle succeeds if:

- 100 qualified projects are identified.
- 30-50 founders are contacted.
- 10 founder calls are booked.
- 5-10 design partners are selected.
- 2-3 projects publicly launch missions.
- At least one launched founder refers another qualified founder.
