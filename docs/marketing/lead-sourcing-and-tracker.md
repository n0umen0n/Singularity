# Lead Sourcing And Tracker

This is the operating workflow for building the first qualified lead list for Singularity's non-crypto primary ICP.

## Lead List Objective

Build a qualified list of **mission-driven projects outside crypto** that are struggling to raise through traditional channels and have no existing token.

The goal is not a large CRM. The goal is to find 20 founders worth scoring in the discovery sprint, 15 worth contacting, and 5 worth speaking with.

See [`discovery-sprint-non-crypto.md`](discovery-sprint-non-crypto.md) for the 2-week validation sprint targets.

## Source Buckets

### X Search

Search for founders and projects using combinations of:

- `building in public fundraising`
- `struggling to raise startup`
- `bootstrapped running out of runway`
- `indie SaaS launch`
- `open source sustainability`
- `looking for angel investors`
- `grant rejected`
- `pre-seed slow`
- `mission driven startup`
- `product hunt launch`
- `climate tech startup`
- `education startup building`
- `health tech founder`
- `OSS maintainer burnout`
- `public goods funding`

Prioritize posts where the founder is explaining progress, posting about fundraising difficulty, or recruiting for unfunded work.

Deprioritize crypto-native search terms (`solana`, `token launch`, `DeFi`, etc.) unless sourcing secondary pre-token crypto-adjacent leads.

### X Reply Mining

Use high-signal source posts from:

- Indie hacker and bootstrap founder threads.
- Fundraising struggle and "building in public" threads.
- Product Hunt launch announcement threads.
- Open-source maintainer sustainability threads.
- Climate, health, and education founder showcases.
- Accelerator demo day threads (non-crypto programs).
- Investor or scout posts asking what people are building outside crypto.

The existing outreach agent can help inspect replies and draft context, but the first campaign should remain founder-led and high-touch.

Recommended source post types for x-reply-agent:

- "What's the hardest part of fundraising right now?"
- "Show me what you're building" threads from non-crypto operators
- Product Hunt maker launch threads
- OSS sustainability and maintainer funding threads

### VC Rejection And Slow-Raise Signals

Pull leads from:

- Founders posting about passed-on rounds, slow raises, or extended bootstrapping.
- Indie SaaS and mission-first startups with visible traction but no announced funding.
- YC/Techstars/accelerator alumni who did not close a follow-on round.
- Founders who pivoted from VC track to community or bootstrap models.

### Open Source

Pull leads from:

- GitHub trending repositories with maintainer sustainability gaps.
- Awesome-list maintainers and popular OSS projects without corporate backing.
- Open Collective and similar platforms with active but underfunded projects.
- Maintainer posts about burnout, sponsorship gaps, or needing paid contributors.

### Mission Communities

Use communities where founders already talk about work:

- Climate tech founder networks and Slack groups.
- Education and health tech founder circles.
- Effective Altruism and public-goods builder communities.
- Indie hacker forums and newsletters.
- Product Hunt maker communities.
- University incubators and science accelerator alumni groups.

Do not spam these channels. Use them to identify founders, then reach out personally with context.

### Demo Days Outside Crypto

Pull leads from:

- University incubator demo days.
- Science and deep-tech accelerators.
- Product Hunt featured launches.
- Mission-driven hackathons (non-crypto).
- Research collective showcases.

### Warm Intro Sources

Ask these people for 2-3 founder referrals:

- Non-crypto angels and operator investors.
- Former founders who mentor bootstrapped teams.
- Journalists and newsletter writers who cover underrated builders.
- Open-source program managers and foundation contacts.
- Accelerator operators outside crypto.
- Founder friends in climate, education, health, and indie SaaS.

Intro ask:

> Who is building something real outside crypto, has visible proof of work, is struggling to raise through traditional channels, and would benefit from a public community-backed funding launch — without needing to become a crypto company?

### Secondary: Crypto-Adjacent Pre-Token (Deprioritized)

Use only after non-crypto primary segment is validated or as warm-intro overflow:

- Hackathon alumni without a token launch.
- Grant recipients without a live token.
- Solana and crypto builder communities (see previous sourcing playbook).

## Daily Sourcing Routine

Run this for 7 days during the discovery sprint.

1. Add 3-5 raw leads from non-crypto source buckets.
2. Score each lead using the ICP rubric.
3. Keep only leads scoring 13 or higher with no live token.
4. Write one context note per qualified lead.
5. Contact 2-3 founders per day with the stuck-fundraising angle.
6. Ask every founder who replies for one referral.

## Prioritization Rules

Contact first:

- Founders who recently posted about fundraising struggles, slow raises, or grant rejections.
- Projects with obvious funded work to be done in the next 30-60 days.
- Teams with enough audience to co-market.
- Warm intros from trusted non-crypto operators.
- Open-source projects with real usage and maintainer sustainability pain.

Deprioritize:

- Projects with a live token or mid-token launch.
- Projects whose only visible goal is liquidity or exchange listing.
- Teams with no founder identity or trust surface.
- Crypto-native teams unless pre-token and referred warmly (secondary segment).
- Any lead where the outreach would have to be generic.
