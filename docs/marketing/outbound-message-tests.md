# Outbound Message Tests

This document defines the first outbound campaign for testing Singularity's product-market fit with project founders.

## Campaign Goal

Book founder calls, not signups.

The first test should learn whether founders have urgent contributor-funding pain and whether they will commit to launching a mission publicly.

## Test Structure

Run three message angles in parallel:

1. Contributor funding.
2. Community-aligned raise.
3. Mission launch.

Send each angle to a similar quality batch of leads. Do not judge message performance from mixed-quality audiences.

Recommended first batch:

| Angle | Leads | Target |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Contributor funding | 15 | Founder calls |
| Community-aligned raise | 15 | Founder calls |
| Mission launch | 15 | Founder calls |

Winning angle is the one that creates the highest number of qualified replies and booked calls, not the highest likes or impressions.

## Message Principles

Every message should:

- Reference something specific the project is building.
- Name one likely funding use case.
- Avoid sounding like a generic token launch pitch.
- Ask for a conversation, not a signup.
- Be short enough to work as a DM or reply.
- Make Singularity sound like distribution plus funding infrastructure.

Avoid:

- "Revolutionary", "disrupt", "democratize", and vague crypto language.
- Overpromising fundraising outcomes.
- Legal ownership language.
- Suggesting passive guaranteed returns.
- Sending the same message to every project.

## Angle 1: Contributor Funding

Use when a project mentions contributors, bounties, audits, ecosystem work, content, growth, integrations, or dev help.

### DM

> Saw what you're building with [project]. Are you currently trying to fund contributors, bounties, audits, or early ecosystem work?
>
> Singularity helps projects launch a mission market with treasury funding built in, so builders can request funding through an onchain approval flow.
>
> Worth a 15 min founder call this week? I am looking for a few design partners we can help launch and promote.

### Short Reply

> If the next step is funding contributors or early work, this is exactly what Singularity is built for: launch a mission market, reserve treasury supply, and fund builders onchain.

### Follow-Up

> Quick follow-up. The design partner offer is hands-on: we help shape the mission, first funding request, and launch story. If contributor funding is not a priority right now, no worries.

## Angle 2: Community-Aligned Raise

Use when a project has a community, waitlist, or strong public audience.

### DM

> [Project] already has the kind of mission people can rally around.
>
> One thing we are testing with Singularity: instead of raising only from insiders, a project can launch a mission market where the community backs the mission and helps make treasury funding visible.
>
> Would you be open to a 15 min call? We are choosing a small group of first launch partners.

### Short Reply

> This feels like the kind of mission that could work well as a community-backed market, especially if treasury funding for builders is part of the launch from day one.

### Follow-Up

> The useful test is whether your community would back a concrete mission and then see how funding requests move to contributors. Happy to show the flow if useful.

## Angle 3: Mission Launch

Use when the project has a crisp public mission but the immediate funding pain is not obvious.

### DM

> Your mission for [project] is clear: [mission in plain English].
>
> Singularity turns missions like that into markets with a token, reserved treasury, and funding feed for builders. We are looking for a few early projects to launch with hands-on positioning and distribution support.
>
> Open to a short call this week?

### Short Reply

> This is a clear mission. Singularity is built for turning missions like this into markets with treasury funding for builders built in.

### Follow-Up

> I think the first useful step would be mapping one concrete funding request that would move [project] forward. If that exists, the mission is probably a strong fit.

## Warm Intro Request

Use this with investors, scouts, devrels, and founder friends.

> I am looking for 5-10 design partners for Singularity.
>
> Best fit: Solana or crypto projects with visible proof of work and an active need to fund contributors, bounties, audits, integrations, ecosystem work, or launch support.
>
> Who is building something real and would benefit from a more public onchain funding flow?

## Founder Call Script

Keep calls focused on pain and commitment.

### Opening

> I am not trying to sell a finished product today. I am testing whether Singularity solves a real funding and contributor coordination problem for projects like yours.

### Discovery Questions

1. What is the mission in your own words?
2. What work most needs to happen in the next 30-60 days?
3. Who would do that work?
4. How would you fund that work today?
5. Where does the current process break down?
6. If a public mission market existed, what would make your community actually care?
7. What would make you uncomfortable about launching this publicly?
8. If we helped with positioning and distribution, would you commit to a first mission launch?

### Close

If strong fit:

> I think there is enough here to design a launch around one concrete funding request. Next step would be a 45 min working session where we draft the mission, funding use case, and launch story.

If weak fit:

> This may be too early for a mission launch, but I would still value a referral to one founder who is actively trying to fund contributors or ecosystem work.

## Response Labels

Tag every reply:

- `urgent-pain`: Founder has a live funding or contributor problem.
- `curious`: Founder likes the idea but has no immediate use case.
- `investor-first`: Founder mostly wants passive capital or liquidity.
- `too-early`: No proof of work or community yet.
- `wrong-fit`: Not crypto-native or not mission-based.
- `design-partner`: Founder is willing to co-design a launch.

## Success Metrics

Per message angle:

| Metric | Target |
| --- | ---: |
| Qualified leads contacted | 15 |
| Replies | 4+ |
| Calls booked | 2+ |
| Design partner candidates | 1+ |

Overall first campaign:

| Metric | Target |
| --- | ---: |
| Founders contacted | 45 |
| Qualified replies | 12 |
| Calls booked | 10 |
| Design partner candidates | 5 |

## Decision Rules

After the first 45 contacts:

- If contributor funding books the most calls, narrow the ICP around teams with immediate funded work.
- If community-aligned raise wins, focus on pre-token communities with active audiences.
- If mission launch wins, improve landing and launch storytelling before adding investor features.
- If none wins, the audience is wrong or the pitch is too abstract. Re-interview warm founders before sending more outbound.

Do not scale automation until one message creates repeatable founder conversations.
